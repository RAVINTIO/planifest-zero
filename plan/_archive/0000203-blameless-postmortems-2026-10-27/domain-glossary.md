# Domain Glossary - blameless postmortems

**Skill:** [plan](../skills/ravintio-plan/SKILL.md)
**Feature:** 0000203-blameless-postmortems
**Version:** 0.25.0

## Terms

| Term | Definition | Aliases | Used In |
|------|-----------|---------|---------|
| Postmortem | A written record of why a failure got through earlier phases. It holds a timeline, a 5 whys chain, and actions. | none | ravintio-zero |
| Blameless | A rule that a cause names a gap in a process, check, standard, or tool. It never names a person or an agent. | none | ravintio-zero |
| 5 whys | A chain of why questions. Each answer becomes the next question until the chain reaches a gap that the pack can close. | none | ravintio-zero |
| Action | A corrective, preventive, or detective change that a postmortem files. Each names its owner, where it lives, and what verifies it. | none | ravintio-zero |
