# Changelog (0000247-git-state-hygiene, 28 Oct 2026)

**Feature:** Git state hygiene
**Version:** 0.36.0
**PR:** #65

## What changed

- `ravintio-orchestrator/SKILL.md` and `ravintio-ship/SKILL.md` tell a step of an active initiative to stage by explicit path and leave other `plan/backlog/` entries untracked (entry 0000213).
- `reserve-id.mjs --commit` commits `plan/backlog/.next-id` alone. The initiative, retrospective, and postmortem skills pass the flag (entry 0000224, ADR-001).
- `agent-dispatch-standards.md` and its skeleton prompts forbid `git stash`, `git checkout`, and `git reset` in a subagent during a parallel batch. The parent's resume stash stays (entry 0000231).
- Three tests: `test-0000247-req-001-stage-by-path.sh`, `test-0000247-req-002-commit-id-reservations.sh`, `test-0000247-req-003-no-stash-in-batch.sh`.
- `docs/architecture-overview.md` and `docs/decisions-index.md` describe the rules.

## Why

Initiative 0000195 hit all three faults. A step staged queued entries, a session left an id reservation uncommitted, and a subagent stashed its siblings' work.
