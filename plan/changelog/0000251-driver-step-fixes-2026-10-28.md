# Changelog (0000251-driver-step-fixes, 28 Oct 2026)

**Feature:** Driver step fixes
**Version:** 0.39.1
**PR:** #69

## What changed

- `run-tests.sh` and `run-tests.ps1` clear `RAVINTIO_INITIATIVE_STEP` before they start any suite (entry 0000229). A suite that needs the variable sets it for its own hook call.
- `initiative-run.mjs` renames an existing `step-{n}.log` to the next free `step-{n}.attempt-{k}.log` before it opens a fresh log (entry 0000235). A rerun keeps every earlier attempt, and the latest attempt keeps the plain name.
- A stop line that names a log also names the earlier attempts, as `Earlier: {paths}. Log: {path}`.
- The symlink refusal covers an attempt path. Attempt files keep mode 600.
- `roadmap-standards.md`, the driver header, `tests/README.md`, `docs/architecture-overview.md`, and `docs/dependency-graph.md` describe the change.
- `test-0000151-req-012-roadmap-step-logs.sh` case (a) asserts the new rule in place of "a rerun truncates the log".
- Two tests: `test-0000251-req-001-runners-clear-step-variable.sh` and `test-0000251-req-002-keep-attempt-logs.sh`.

## Why

In initiative 0000195, hook tests failed in three steps because the step variable reached them. The first step 13 session also left no evidence once the driver re-ran the step, because the driver truncated the log.

## Deviations

None. The running driver predates this change, so a re-run of a step under that driver still truncates the log. The human restarts the driver and the watcher to load the new code.
